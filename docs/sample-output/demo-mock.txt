Q: What is transitive dependency in 3NF?
A relation is in third normal form when it is in 2NF and no non-prime attribute is transitively dependent on a candidate key. [1] If roll_no -> dept_id and dept_id -> dept_name, then dept_name is transitively dependent on roll_no and should move to a separate department table. [1] Always check that the decomposition is lossless and, for 3NF, dependency preserving. [3]

Summary of os-cpu-scheduling.md:
**Summary**
- Good scheduling improves CPU utilisation and throughput and reduces waiting time, turnaround time and response time.
- Turnaround time = completion time - arrival time.
- Waiting time = turnaround time - burst time.
- Response time = time of first CPU allocation - arrival time.

**Key terms:** time, processes, process, waiting, priority, scheduling, turnaround, long
