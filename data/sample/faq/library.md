# Central library

## Library timings
The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays and from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on Saturdays. During end-semester exams the reading hall stays open 24x7. The library is closed on Sundays and national holidays.

## Borrowing books
Undergraduate students can borrow up to 4 books for 14 days. Postgraduate and PhD scholars can borrow up to 8 books for 30 days. Books can be renewed twice online through the library OPAC if no one has reserved them.

## Library fine
The overdue fine is Rs 2 per book per day. Lost books must be replaced with the same edition or paid for at twice the current price. The no-dues certificate needed before the final semester results is issued only after all fines are cleared.

## E-resources and remote access
Students get remote access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Springer and NPTEL course material through the library proxy. Log in with your institute email ID. Plagiarism checks for project reports are done at the library help desk.
