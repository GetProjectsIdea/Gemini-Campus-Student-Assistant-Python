# Examinations and attendance

## Minimum attendance
Students need at least 75% attendance in every course to be allowed to sit the end-semester exam. Medical leave supported by a certificate submitted within 7 days can be condoned up to 10%, subject to approval by the Head of Department.

## Mid-semester and end-semester exams
Each course has two mid-semester tests (20 marks each, best one counted), 20 marks of internal assessment and a 60-mark end-semester exam. The exam timetable is published on the student portal at least 3 weeks before the exams begin.

## Revaluation
Students can apply for revaluation of an end-semester answer sheet within 7 days of the result. The revaluation fee is Rs 500 per course. A photocopy of the answer sheet can be requested for Rs 300 per course.

## Supplementary exams
Students who fail a course can take the supplementary exam held in July. Registration for supplementary exams opens on the student portal two weeks after the end-semester results.
