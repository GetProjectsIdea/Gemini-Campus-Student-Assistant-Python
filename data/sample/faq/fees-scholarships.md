# Fees and scholarships

## Fee payment deadline
The semester fee must be paid on the student portal before the 15th of the first month of the semester. A late fee of Rs 100 per day is charged for up to 15 days, after which the registration is put on hold.

## Scholarships
Students can apply for central and state government scholarships through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). The institute's own merit scholarship gives a 50% tuition waiver to the top 5% of each branch based on the previous year's CGPA. Scholarship documents are verified by the scholarship cell in the admin block, room 12.

## Education loan documents
The accounts section issues a fee structure letter and a bonafide certificate for education loans within 3 working days. Request both on the student portal under "Certificates".
