# Hostel rules and timings

## Hostel gate timings
The hostel main gate closes at 10:00 PM for first-year students and at 10:30 PM for second, third and final-year students. Late entry after the gate closes needs a late pass from the warden, which must be requested on the hostel portal before 6:00 PM on the same day.

## Mess timings
Breakfast is served from 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM, lunch from 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM, evening snacks from 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM and dinner from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM. On Sundays breakfast starts at 8:00 AM.

## Hostel leave and night out
Students going home for a weekend or holiday must fill the leave form on the hostel portal at least 24 hours in advance. Parents receive an SMS when the leave is approved by the warden.

## Wi-Fi in hostel
Hostel Wi-Fi uses the campus login (roll number and portal password). Wi-Fi is switched off between 1:00 AM and 6:00 AM in first-year blocks. For connectivity problems raise a ticket with the IT helpdesk at extension 2244.
