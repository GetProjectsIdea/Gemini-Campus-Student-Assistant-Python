# Training and placement

## Placement eligibility
Students with a CGPA of 6.0 or above and no active backlogs can register for campus placements in their final year. Some companies set a higher cut-off, which is mentioned in each job notice.

## One offer policy
Once a student accepts a job offer through the placement cell, they cannot sit for another company in the same placement season, except for dream companies with a CTC above Rs 12 LPA.

## Internships
The Training and Placement Office (TPO) shares internship openings on the placement portal. Summer internships of 6 to 8 weeks after the third year are counted for 2 credits if the student submits a report and an internship certificate.

## Placement training
Aptitude and coding practice sessions are held every Wednesday from 3:00 PM to 4:00 PM in the auditorium. Mock interviews with alumni are scheduled in July.
