# Student support and helpdesk

## Anti-ragging
Ragging is a punishable offence under the UGC regulations. Students can report ragging confidentially to the anti-ragging squad, the national anti-ragging helpline 1800-180-5522 or by email to the Dean of Student Welfare.

## Counselling
A professional student counsellor is available in the medical centre from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Appointments are confidential and can be booked at the medical centre reception.

## IT helpdesk
For portal password resets, email account issues and Wi-Fi problems, contact the IT helpdesk in the CSE block ground floor or call extension 2244 between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

## Bonafide certificate
A bonafide certificate can be requested on the student portal under "Certificates". It is issued by the academic section within 2 working days.
